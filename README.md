# SAC Templates Hub

**Free, ready-to-use templates for SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC) and SAC Planning.**

🔗 **Website: [https://sactemplateshub.com](https://sactemplateshub.com)**

SAC Templates Hub is an independent project that gives SAP Analytics Cloud consultants and finance teams a head start: 64 ready-made templates with KPIs, dimensions and realistic sample data, so you build a Story or Planning model in minutes instead of days. No account required, no sign-up — download as `.xlsx`, `.csv` or SAC package and import.

> Independent project, not affiliated with SAP. SAP and SAP Analytics Cloud are trademarks of SAP SE.

## What's inside

- **64 free templates** across 16 industries — finance, banking, insurance, retail, supply chain, ESG, HR and more.
- **A rule-based template generator** — describe a use case, get a structured starting model.
- **Interactive KPI previews** — see the dimensions and measures before you download.
- **Regulatory accelerators** for Solvency II, Basel III and CSRD — thresholds pre-filled, import guide included (paid).
- **A practical blog** with guides on SAC Planning, aggregation, the Version dimension, IFRS 17, IFRS 9 ECL, CSRD/ESRS and tool comparisons (SAC vs Power BI, SAC vs Anaplan).

SAP Analytics Cloud consultants, freelancers and agencies who deliver fast, and finance/compliance teams who want a structured starting point they configure and validate themselves.
